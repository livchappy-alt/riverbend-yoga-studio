Yoga That Meets You Where You Are

Riverbend Yoga Studio is a welcoming place for people who want to add movement and mindfulness to their everyday lives. Our classes are designed for beginners, busy professionals, older adults, and anyone who would like to practice yoga without feeling intimidated.

Our Mission

Our goal is simple: help people feel more comfortable in their bodies and give them time to pause during busy days. We believe yoga should be adaptable to the person practicing it, not the other way around. Our instructors encourage students to listen to their bodies and make modifications whenever they are needed.

Never Tried Yoga Before?

That is completely okay. You do not have to be flexible, experienced, or familiar with yoga poses before coming to Riverbend. Beginners are encouraged to ask questions, take breaks, and work at a comfortable pace. Our instructors are here to guide you through the class rather than expect you to already know what to do.

Featured Class: Restorative Yoga

Restorative Yoga is a slower class focused on relaxation, comfortable positions, and breathing. It can be a good starting point for someone new to yoga or a peaceful option for someone who needs a break from a busy week.
