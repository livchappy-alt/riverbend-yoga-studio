Accessibility and Accommodations

We want students to feel comfortable asking for an adjustment. Instructors can offer alternative poses, movement modifications, additional support, or the option to rest when needed.

If you have an accessibility concern or would like to discuss an accommodation before your first class, please contact the studio. We would rather answer your questions ahead of time so you can arrive feeling prepared.

Studio Policies

  • Please arrive a few minutes before class begins.
  • Wear clothing that allows you to move comfortably.
  • Let your instructor know if you need a modification.
  • Students should practice within their own comfort level.
  • Please help us maintain a calm and respectful studio environment.
  • Questions about accommodations are always welcome.
