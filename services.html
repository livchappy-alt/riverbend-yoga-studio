Find Your Practice

There is no single right way to practice yoga. Riverbend offers several class styles so students can choose something that feels appropriate for their goals and experience.

Group Classes

Gentle Yoga

Gentle Yoga uses slower movements and simple poses. The class is especially welcoming to beginners and older adults, but anyone who prefers a quieter pace is welcome.

Vinyasa Yoga

Vinyasa connects movement with breathing in a flowing sequence. Students can choose modifications throughout class and are encouraged to work at their own pace.

Restorative Yoga

Restorative Yoga gives students time to settle into supported poses and focus on relaxation. It is a good choice when your goal is to slow down rather than have an intense workout.

Private Yoga Sessions

Private sessions offer one-on-one instruction for students who want additional guidance. An instructor can adjust the session around your goals, experience level, and preferred pace.

Private sessions can also be helpful for students who would like to learn modifications before joining a group class.

Pricing Overview

Group classes are offered at a standard class rate, while private sessions are priced according to the length of the session. Workshop pricing may vary depending on the topic and length of the event.

Riverbend does not require online checkout. New students can contact the studio first to ask questions, compare options, and decide which class or service is a good fit.

Not Sure Where to Start?

If you have never practiced yoga before, Gentle Yoga is a comfortable place to begin. You can also contact us before attending and tell us about your experience, schedule, or concerns. We are happy to help you find an option that feels right.
